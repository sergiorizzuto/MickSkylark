About the Film

A comedy about second chances, unlikely mentors, and the world's most underrated sport.

Mick Skylark, coached by his mentor Jonsey, quickly rises to the top as a child mini golf prodigy. When a tragedy strikes that he feels responsible for, he quits the sport for good.
🎭 Comedy 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family Friendly ⛳ Sports ❤️ Heartfelt

The Story

Mick Skylark isn't your typical sports hero. He's a kid with a gift — an almost supernatural ability to read greens, judge angles, and sink impossible putts. Under the wing of his eccentric mentor Jonsey, Mick tears through the competitive mini golf circuit, becoming the youngest prodigy the sport has ever seen.

But when tragedy strikes — one that Mick believes is entirely his fault — he walks away from the game, his putter, and everything that once made him feel alive. Years pass. The trophies collect dust. The greens grow over without him.

Until life, as it tends to do, pulls him back in.

"At its heart, Mick Skylark is a story about guilt, faith, forgiveness, and learning that sometimes you don't get what you want, but you get what you need."

Tone & Inspiration

Mick Skylark lives in the sweet spot between laugh-out-loud comedy and genuine heart. It's a story of redemption, justice, and faith, all while being fun for everyone.

The film draws inspiration from the golden age of sports comedies: the irreverence of Dodgeball, the wild energy of Happy Gilmore, the lovable weirdness of Kingpin, and the timeless silliness of Young Frankenstein. It's PG to PG-13, designed for the whole family to enjoy together.

The Production

Mick Skylark: Mini Golf Prodigy is written and directed by Sergio Rizzuto, co-written by Jett Miller, and produced by Sergio Rizzuto, Tyler Jon Olson, and John Michaels. The film is a Potato Eater Productions project, brought to life by a team passionate about making people laugh — and maybe shed a tear or two.

As featured in Deadline, the film has already generated buzz for its standout cast and fresh take on the sports comedy genre.
