⛳ Executive Summary & Recommendations

51 respondents from the Boca Raton test screening provided overwhelmingly positive feedback. The film averages an 8.0/10 overall rating, an 8.0/10 humor score, and an exceptional 9.2/10 "glad I saw it" score. 80% of the audience would recommend the film to a friend (4 or 5 out of 5). The emotional tone is strongly positive — Happy (69%), Amused (59%), and Moved/Emotional (35%) dominate. Only 2% reported feeling bored.

The songs were a major hit, averaging 9.0/10. The ending landed well with 88% loving or liking it. Dr. Phil (Warburton) and Humphrey are clear audience favorites, with Vanessa's emotional arc resonating strongly in the third act.

Top Strengths

  • Dr. Phil / Warburton character universally beloved
  • Humphrey is a comedy engine — hamster wheel & turkey leg are standouts
  • Vanessa's emotional arc and the money reveal are deeply satisfying
  • Songs rated 9.0/10 average
  • Mini golf commentators consistently funny
  • Strong "feel good" message — 88% confirmed uplifting
  • Film is very easy to follow (82% said yes, 18% mostly)
  • Musket gag and physical comedy land well (with caveats)

Areas to Tighten

  • Bowling scenes — mentioned 6+ times as least favorite
  • Lightning/cloud VFX — needs visual improvement
  • Crystal / girlfriend subplot — feels unnecessary to many
  • Pacing in middle act — tournament and lab scenes drag
  • Musket gag repetition — funny once or twice, overdone after
  • Dumpster turkey / skydiver scenes — polarizing
  • Fade-to-black transitions — too many, breaks flow
  • Lily resolution — needs better closure
