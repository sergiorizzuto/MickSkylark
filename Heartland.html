About the film

Mick Skylark (Brandon Engman), coached by his mentor Jonsey (David Koechner), quickly rises to the top as a child mini golf prodigy. When a tragedy strikes and he feels responsible, he quits the sport for good. Also featuring Patrick Warburton, Elizabeth Mitchell, Thomas Lennon, Robert Knepper, and Jorge Garcia.
