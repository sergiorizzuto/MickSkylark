⚖️ Screening Terms & Conditions

Please read before attending

🎬 Test Screener Notice

This is an unfinished test screening of "Mick Skylark: Mini Golf Prodigy."

The purpose of this screening is to gather valuable audience feedback to help us improve the film before its final release. This is a work-in-progress, and certain elements (including visual effects, sound mixing, color grading, and editing) may not be complete.

By attending, you agree to:

  • Fill out a post-screening survey to provide constructive feedback
  • Understand this is an unfinished version of the film
  • Help us make the film better with your honest opinions
  • Provide feedback on story, characters, pacing, and overall experience

Your feedback is crucial to our filmmaking process. Thank you for being part of our test audience!

📵 No Recording Policy

NO RECORDING OF ANY KIND IS PERMITTED.

This includes but is not limited to:

  • Video recording
  • Audio recording
  • Photography
  • Screen captures
  • Live streaming

This policy applies to all screenings on February 22, 2026 and any future screenings. Violators will be asked to leave immediately.

🎟️ RSVP & Admission

Seating is first-come, first-served. RSVPs do not guarantee entry if the venue reaches capacity. Please arrive early to secure your seat.

RSVPs are non-transferable. The screening is free to attend.

📝 Post-Screening Survey Requirement

All attendees are required to complete a post-screening survey.

As this is a test screening, your feedback is essential to our production process. After the screening, you will receive a survey link to provide your thoughts on:

  • Story and character development
  • Pacing and editing
  • Comedy and emotional moments
  • Overall viewing experience
  • Suggestions for improvement

The survey takes approximately 10-15 minutes to complete. Your honest feedback helps us create the best possible final version of the film.

🎬 Screening Etiquette

We ask that all attendees:

  • Silence mobile devices
  • Refrain from talking during the film
  • Be respectful of other attendees
  • Follow all venue staff instructions

We reserve the right to refuse entry or remove anyone who disrupts the screening experience.
